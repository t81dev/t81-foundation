## Mission, in 90 seconds

T81 Foundation is an **experimental computing research platform**, not a validated ternary CPU or commercial operating system. Its central question is whether balanced ternary, Base-81 grouping, or adaptive representations can beat **matched optimized binary baselines** under explicitly stated workloads, total costs, reliability constraints, and physical substrates.
