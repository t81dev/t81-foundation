### `H06-MEM-10` Negative-Results Note (Closed Path)
- **Tried:** Per-column CSR `uint4` 128-trit block-sparse Metal kernel (`r1_sbdr128_pop_gemm`) across `L = 64` (`4096×1024`), `M ∈ {1, 4, 16}`, `s ∈ {50%, 75%, 90%}` on Apple M2 (`sha3=OK`).
- **Why It Failed:** At `M = 1..4`, 64-layer Metal dispatch latency + indirect `Idx[j]` gather + `< 32` active blocks per column leaving SIMD lanes idle made CSR **slower** than dense `GPU_R1_DualRail_Pop` (`0.67×–0.83×` at `M = 4`) and slower than 1-thread CPU `SBDR128_SIMD` (`0.77×` at `M = 1, s = 90%`).
