## A1 — Proposed §2 host assurance (NON-NORMATIVE)

> Host qualification under F01-HOST-BOLQ-v1 is bounded observed evidence for an
> exact source, host/boot/kernel/manager, interpreter/dependency/control identity,
> declared workload envelope and prospectively fixed finite fault scenarios.
> It establishes no universal scheduling, fault-survival, stop, exit, reaping
> or durability guarantee. Linux mechanism evidence, pre-release admission checks
> and measured outcomes shall be reported as distinct classes. Resource controls
> and monotonic stop mechanisms shall be configured/read back and armed before
> work; periodic memory samples or CPU accounting alone do not establish those
> policies. The configured mechanisms' implementation/scope and actual observations
> shall be named, without claiming that configuration proves every physical bound.
> Unknown or failed mandatory admission prerequisites prohibit release. There
> is no escalation, unqualified-host substitution or uncharged custom observer.

Mechanism evidence means the actual kernel/manager policy and its readbacks,
not an achieved latency. Admission includes unchanged exact-source, sealed
bounded-authorization, dependency/venv, rights, containment/reaper, host and human
checks. Measured evidence includes actual time/CPU, peaks, signal/request/exit/
wait/population and persistence observations with uncertainty. No new exception
to any deterministic pre-side-effect check is proposed by this split.

## A4 — Proposed §9 host evidence and classification (NON-NORMATIVE)

> Preserve raw source/input/control/case evidence, counters and scientific labels.
> Keep host qualification separate, with PASS, FAILED, INCOMPLETE and NOT VERIFIED
> as distinct per-criterion outcomes. PASS requires complete admissible evidence
> meeting every mandatory assertion in its exact declared scope. FAILED records
> a conclusive violation and is sticky despite other missing evidence. INCOMPLETE
> means attempted but missing/interrupted/insufficient evidence or uncertainty
> straddling the threshold with no established violation. NOT VERIFIED means
> not attempted/authorized/admitted or outside the approved applicability scope.
> An admission failure does not imply the prohibited operation was run.
> Rollup cannot hide mandatory FAILED, INCOMPLETE or NOT VERIFIED criteria in a
> PASS. Report operation completion and evidence completeness separately.
>
> Fix scenario assertions, fault scope, thresholds, uncertainty rules and any
> exclusions before tests. PASS requires a justified upper measurement bound
> within the criterion; a justified lower bound above it is FAILED. A straddling
> interval is INCOMPLETE for that comparison. Never widen uncertainty or alter
> coverage after results to rescue a failure. Raw point estimates and sampling
> gaps remain visible. Finite passing samples do not prove a future maximum.
>
> The controller alone finalizes after confirmed helper stop/reaping, within its
> original allowance. Death, blocked I/O, lost rights or unknown reaping prevents
> a successful completion claim. Preserve confirmed checkpoints, partial tails
> and ordinary existing manager records with actual collection/persistence scope.
> Manager termination promises no new terminal receipt, seal, flush or post-kill
> writer. Missing durability evidence is INCOMPLETE; an observed persistence
> failure is FAILED. Successful local filesystem operations do not establish an
> untested power-loss guarantee or the modeled Area E journal claim.

An intentionally injected controller/guard death may PASS its predeclared abort-
response assertion only with all required timing/exit/wait evidence, while the
interrupted provisioning/experimental operation remains incomplete. Cleanup
success never substitutes for a complete installation or scientific result.
A known overrun plus missing receipt is FAILED with incomplete evidence, not a
rescued INCOMPLETE resource outcome. Unexercised OOM, starvation, blocked-operation
or authority-loss classes remain visible as NOT VERIFIED; mandatory missing
coverage blocks the requested scope, rather than being silently excluded.
