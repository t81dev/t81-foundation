# Proposed F coverage amendment — NOT APPLIED / NOT AUTHORIZED

The existing protocol and PR #805 remain byte-identical. This document records
a proposed human decision, not a specification update or execution permission.

The retained F generator has 288 inputs, already more than its 256-state cap.
With the current counting rules and mandatory controls its full path costs
1,760 states. Stopping after the structural contradiction can answer a bounded
satisfiability question, but cannot be described as exhaustive fixture coverage.
The harness therefore blocks F instead of silently dropping cases or controls.

One possible separate amendment would retain the scientific question—whether
any manifest can satisfy unchanged D3 and the required binding field—and make
the primary experiment a structural key-set proof with a separately reviewed
literal corpus of at most 16 comparison inputs plus the existing mandatory
controls and their four rotations. At six charged units per input plus 32
mandatory-control states, that proposed plan would reserve at most 128 states.
The original 288-input cross product would be explicitly removed from that
amended claim and retained as an unexecuted, separately budgeted future check.
Fixture selection and sufficiency require independent review; no such corpus
is selected, generated or substituted here. The current generator is unchanged.

Alternatively, retaining exhaustive 288-input coverage needs a separately
approved budget amendment: at least 1,760 F states plus any added instrumentation.
An increased F allowance cannot be added on top of the unchanged 49,408 suite
ceiling. Redistribution requires its own impact review, particularly because
A/C/D/E already cannot complete their full paths under their original caps.
No allocation transfer is proposed as automatically safe or implemented.

Before either option could be used: obtain an explicit human protocol decision,
independently review domains/counting/controls and negative evidence, publish
a new protocol revision/hash, update and review the harness identities, qualify
Linux, and obtain separate execution authorization. This proposal grants none
of those actions. The original suite-wide ceilings remain unchanged here.
